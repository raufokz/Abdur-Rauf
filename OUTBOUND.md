for nothing.

## 3. Cold email — real estate businesses

Send to realtors, brokerages and investors whose site has a visible, specific problem. Do not
send to anyone whose site you have not actually opened.

### Version A — the site is slow or broken

**Subject:** `your listings page takes 9 seconds on mobile`

```
Hi {Name},

I opened {site} on my phone this morning — the listings page took about {N} seconds to become
usable. On mobile, most property searches are abandoned well before that.

I checked and it looks like {specific cause: uncompressed hero images / a page builder loading
on every page / render-blocking scripts}. That is usually a few hours of work, not a rebuild.

I build real estate websites and property search platforms — domesticrealestate.us and
iproply.com are two of mine.

Want me to send a short written breakdown of what is slowing it down? Free, no obligation, and
useful whether or not you hire me.

Abdur Rauf
Full Stack Developer | rauforakzai10@gmail.com
https://raufokz.github.io/Abdur-Rauf/
```

### Version B — their lead capture is weak

**Subject:** `one form field is costing you leads on {site}`

```
Hi {Name},

Quick observation on {site}: your contact form asks for {N} fields up front, and buyers and
sellers get the same one.

I worked as an ISA on US real estate campaigns before I was a developer, so I have made the
calls on the other end of forms like this. Two things reliably help: splitting buyer and seller
into separate paths, and staging the questions instead of asking everything at once.

I built exactly that for omnireferral.us — a four-step intake that qualifies the lead, then
routes it by ZIP to the right agent.

If useful, I can map out what I would change on yours in a short reply. No charge.

Abdur Rauf
Full Stack Developer | rauforakzai10@gmail.com
https://raufokz.github.io/Abdur-Rauf/
```

### Follow-up (once, after 4–5 days)

```
Hi {Name},

Following up on the note about {specific thing}. If it is not a priority right now, no problem —
just say and I will stop emailing.

If it is useful later, everything I have built is at https://raufokz.github.io/Abdur-Rauf/

Abdur
```

**Send once, follow up once, then stop.** A third email converts almost nobody and costs you the
domain reputation.

## 4. Weekly rhythm

Volume is the variable most people get wrong — they do a burst, get no reply, and conclude
outbound does not work.

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Mon | 5 Upwork proposals — only posts you can name a matching project for |
| Tue | 10 cold emails, each one with a specific observation about their site |
| Wed | 1 LinkedIn post — something you fixed, a trade-off you made, a short before/after |
| Thu | 5 Upwork proposals + follow-ups on last week's emails |
| Fri | Update one case study or add one screenshot to the portfolio |

Expect a low reply rate. That is normal, not a signal to stop. What separates outbound that works
from outbound that does not is almost always specificity — a named observation about *their* site
beats any amount of polish about yours.

## 5. Tracking

Track outcomes somewhere, or you cannot tell which channel is worth the hours:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Date | 2026-08-18 |
| Channel | Cold email / Upwork / LinkedIn / Referral |
| Target | brokerage name + URL |
| Angle used | slow listings page |
| Replied? | Y / N |
| Outcome | no reply / call booked / quoted / won / lost |

After ~50 attempts you will see which channel and which angle actually produce replies. Then do
more of that and stop the rest.
