About me

A developer who learned the business side first

I started my working life as an office boy. In the evenings, after everyone had gone home, I taught myself HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PHP on the office computers. That habit turned into a junior developer role, then a developer role, and eventually into leading technical operations for a team.

Along the way I spent time as an ISA and real estate call agent on United States campaigns — qualifying leads, working CRMs, VoIP and SMS systems. That is an unusual background for a developer, and it is why I understand what a lead is worth before I design the form that captures it.

Key strengths

  • Clean, maintainable, well-commented code
  • Team player — I lead and interview developers
  • Written scope and fixed quote before any code
  • Ownership after launch, not just at handover
  • Business questions before feature lists

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What services do you offer?

Custom web applications in Laravel, PHP, React and Angular; WordPress and WooCommerce builds; REST API development and integration; admin dashboards; real estate websites and lead systems; GoHighLevel and CRM automation; technical SEO and performance work; and ongoing website maintenance.

How do you work with clients?

A short discovery call, then a written scope with milestones and a fixed quote before any code is written.

Development runs with regular plain-English updates, followed by a review round against the agreed scope, deployment with hosting, DNS and SSL configured, and a support window after launch.

What is your availability?

I work as Technical Operations Team Lead at AESYRIX and take selected freelance projects alongside it. I'm open to contract work, monthly retainers and full-time remote roles. I work from Pakistan Standard Time with regular overlap into US and UK business hours.

How can we start a project?

Send the details through the contact form, by email to rauforakzai10@gmail.com, or on WhatsApp at +92 370 1913453. Describe the problem rather than the feature list and my first reply will be more useful.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. I work remotely with international clients, including United States real estate businesses. Communication is in English by email, WhatsApp or scheduled calls.

Do you take over projects another developer started?

Yes, and it's a large part of what I do. I'll audit the existing codebase or site first and tell you honestly whether it's cheaper to repair or rebuild, before either of us commits to anything.

Who owns the code and accounts when we're done?

You do. Repositories, hosting, domains and third-party accounts are set up in your name or transferred to you at handover. I don't hold client work hostage.
