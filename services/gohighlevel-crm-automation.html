Service

GoHighLevel & CRM automation

Pipelines, funnels and follow-up sequences configured so that a captured lead is actually worked — not stored.

What I set up

  • GoHighLevel onboarding — account and sub-account configuration from scratch
  • Pipelines — stages that reflect how your business actually qualifies and closes
  • Funnels and landing pages — capture pages connected to the pipeline, not floating separately
  • SMS and email workflows — follow-up sequences with sensible timing and exit conditions
  • Lead routing and notification — the right person alerted fast enough to matter
  • CRM customisation — custom fields, modules and workflow logic, including Perfex CRM
  • Integrations — website forms, calendars, phone systems and third-party APIs
  • Automation cleanup — untangling workflows that fire twice, contradict each other or silently stopped working

Where automation usually goes wrong

Sequences written for the sender, not the recipient

Five messages in three days reads as pressure and gets you marked as spam. Timing is a deliverability decision as much as a marketing one.

No exit conditions

A lead who has already replied should not keep receiving "just checking in" messages. Every sequence needs defined exits, and most broken setups I inspect are missing them.

Pipeline stages that describe hope

Stages should describe observable facts, not optimism. "Interested" is not a stage — "call booked" is. If a stage cannot be verified, your pipeline report is fiction.

Nobody watching whether it still works

An integration breaks quietly. Six weeks later someone notices leads stopped arriving. I set up checks so failures surface immediately.
